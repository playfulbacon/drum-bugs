DrumBugs

·

Teach a bug a rhythm. It loops that rhythm forever.
Its routine is its drum part — so building the workforce is writing the song.

  • Bugs come out of the nest. Pick one and drop it in the lit clearing. You go straight into teaching.
  • Drum the routine with W A S D — north, west, south, east — and space to act. The routine loops while you build it, so keep going round until it's right. Nothing commits until you confirm.
  • Aim by walking into things. A move always turns the bug, even when the way is blocked — so nose into a rock, then act.
  • Aim for a loop that closes — one that comes back to where it started. A routine that drifts is a bug that travels; re-teach it when it arrives.
  • Then conduct. 123 cue sections in and out, QWE nudge them a beat late, ASD accent their next action. On the beat it lands now; off the beat it waits for the bar.
  • Get every berry to the nest. Ants carry. Beetles smash rock. Crickets jump water, and kick the berry they're standing on.
