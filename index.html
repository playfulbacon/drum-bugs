DrumBugs

·

Bugs walk the forest floor in silence.
What they walk over makes the sound.

  • Click an empty lane to set a bug walking. It turns around at the edges.
  • Click a lane with a bug to make it stomp — a loud hit on whatever it's standing on.
  • Stomp on the beat. Off-beat still works, it's just a thud. Land it and you get a crack.
  • Seeds lie around. Stomp one to crack it open where it is — or let the bug pick it up and carry it somewhere better.
  • Stomping while carrying plants a flower. The ground decides what grows.

Keys 1–5 stomp lanes top to bottom · shift-click or right-click removes a bug

Headphones recommended.
