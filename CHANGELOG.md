# Changelog

## v0.1 — "first crawl"

First playable. Everything we settled on in the concept conversation, and
nothing beyond it.

**In:**
- 13×5 lane field of forest-floor terrain: dry leaves, puddles, hollow logs,
  stones, moss, soft soil, bare earth. Clustered generation so it reads as a
  place rather than a grid of samples.
- Three bugs with different periods — snail (every 3 beats), beetle (every 2),
  cricket (every beat). Polyrhythm falls out for free.
- Bugs are silent while walking except for a soft brush against whatever they
  cross. Bare ground is silence.
- **Stomp**, on a tap. Quantised to the nearest beat, with a reward gradient and
  no failure state: *thud* → *good* → *CRACK!*
- Seeds lie scattered. Stomp one to crack it in place; leave it and the bug
  carries it. Stomping while carrying plants a flower and still makes the loud
  hit — the tap never stops being a musical act.
- Flowers are the only pitched layer (C major pentatonic, one degree per lane).
  Kind is decided by the ground you plant on: dew on water, pod on wood or dry
  leaf, crystal on stone, bloom on soil and moss.
- A gust every 8 bars blows in new seeds.
- Synthesised foley throughout — no samples, no external assets.

**Deliberately not in yet:**
- The wear/bloom ecology (pads eroding under heavy play, quiet ground seeding
  itself). Parked until the core loop has been played.
- Moulting, bug hunger, day/night.
- Anchors for pinning parts of a pattern.
- Saving or sharing a field.
