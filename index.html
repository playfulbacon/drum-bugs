DrumBugs

·

Teach a bug a rhythm. It loops that rhythm forever.
Its routine is its drum part — so building the workforce is writing the song.

  • Pick a bug and click the garden to place it. You go straight into teaching.
  • Drum the routine with W A D space — step, turn left, turn right, act. Not pressing anything leaves a rest. Watch the path preview build as you go.
  • Aim for a loop that closes — one that comes back to where it started, facing the same way. The badge tells you.
  • Then conduct. 123 cue sections in and out, QWE nudge them a beat late, ASD accent their next action. On the beat it lands now; off the beat it waits for the bar.
  • Get every berry to the nest. Ants carry. Beetles smash rock. Crickets jump water and kick berries.
